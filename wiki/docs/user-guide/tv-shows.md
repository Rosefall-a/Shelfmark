# TV shows

TV shows track series metadata, seasons, episodes, watch state, ratings, lists, activity, relations, and recommendations.

Add a show manually or from configured metadata search. On the detail page, add or edit seasons, update individual episodes, or mark a season's episodes watched in bulk. Airing metadata can be refreshed for one show and by the administrator-controlled scheduled airing job.

Deleting a show moves it to trash. Restore returns the show and its season/episode data; purge permanently removes it. Provider searches and refreshes require the administrator to configure the relevant credentials.
